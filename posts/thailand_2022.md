Our third trip to South East Asia, and we packed a lot into it. Bangkok first, then Koh Samui, then Chiang Mai.

## Bangkok

We started at the Chatuchak Weekend Market, which sprawls further than you expect. We spent most of it eating coconut ice cream and mango sticky rice. It was also where we met Thailand’s short and sudden torrential rain for the first time. Everyone scrambled for cover at once, which was quite the spectacle.

<img src="/bk_sticky_rice.jpeg" class="image fit">

Afterwards we walked through Chatuchak Park watching locals enjoy their day, then headed back to our hotel in the MBK Center. Hotels inside malls are a common thing in Bangkok, and MBK is a massive complex with around 3,000 stores. Most of them closed early though, maybe another Covid rule, so dinner ended up being instant noodles at the hotel.

Day two started with castella, a very good sponge cake, before a tour of Bangkok’s temples. The architecture is intricate and the grounds are calm in a way the rest of the city isn’t. We finished in Chinatown with dim sum.

<img src="/castella.jpeg" class="image fit">
<img src="/bk_temple.jpeg" class="image fit">

Day three was Lumphini Park, where the monitor lizards wander around freely, and then the tower in Silom, where we braved the glass floor. Looking straight down at the city through your own feet is thrilling and terrifying in roughly equal measure.

Day four was the Reclining Buddha and the sunset over Wat Arun. For dinner we tried the Michelin-starred street food from the famous “goggles grandma”. Long wait, worth it. We finished with an hour-long massage that cost almost nothing. Let me tell you, Thai women are strong.

## Koh Samui

We flew to Koh Samui on the fifth day. The island’s airport feels more like a mini-resort than an airport, which set the tone nicely. Our hotel’s location was a bit eerie at night though, with dogs barking constantly.

<img src="/samui_airport.jpeg" class="image fit">
<img src="/samui.jpeg" class="image fit">

The next day we took a road trip around the island. The highlight was feeding and bathing elephants, which is as good as it sounds.

<img src="/elepha_samui.jpeg" class="image fit">

Day three was an island-hopping and snorkelling tour. The waves were rough and my swimming turned out to be insufficient, so I had to be rescued by an Australian. Embarrassing at the time, decent story now.

<img src="/island_hopping_samui.jpeg" class="image fit">

Day four was go-karting, followed by the best bao buns I have ever eaten.

## Chiang Mai

We flew to Chiang Mai on the fifth day. Beautiful landscapes, and rain that did not let up.

<img src="/chiang_mai.jpeg" class="image fit">

On our second day there we flew back to Bangkok for one last outing, a halal food festival and a fruit festival right outside the Diamond Mall. We got hit by another torrential downpour, which by that point felt about right.

<img src="/bk_food_festival.jpeg" class="image fit">

P.S. If you’re in Bangkok, get hold of a Dark Dino. It’s just amazing.
